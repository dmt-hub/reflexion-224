WCS program flow

One 224X/224XL or original 224 program, row by row, and how values move between its rows: from delay memory into a register, into a multiply, through the accumulator into RR, and back into delay memory or out to the DAC. Hover a row (click to pin) to see where its values come from and where they go. The program is read in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
