# reflexion-224

An emulation of the Lexicon 224X and 224XL digital reverbs that runs the
machines' own firmware in your browser. You supply the ROMs; they stay in
your browser.

Work in progress: the rest of the project, and its documentation, will
follow here.

Lexicon, 224, 224X and 224XL are named only to say what is emulated. This
project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lexicon or its owners.
